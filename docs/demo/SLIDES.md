## Presentation Tips

### Timing
- 2 minutes per slide
- Total: 20 minutes
- Allow 10 minutes for Q&A
- Total session: 30 minutes

### Delivery Best Practices
1. **Start with impact:** Open with a compelling statistic
2. **Show, don't tell:** Use live demo heavily (Slides 5-6)
3. **Address concerns proactively:** Privacy & compliance (Slide 9)
4. **End with clear action:** Make next steps obvious (Slide 10)

### Technical Setup
- Test screen sharing 5 minutes early
- Have backup screenshots in case of technical issues
- Keep demo environment open and ready
- Prepare for common objections

### Audience Engagement
- Pause for questions after Slide 6 (post-demo)
- Ask: "What safety challenges is your school facing?"
- Tailor examples to their specific context
- Use their school name in examples when possible

### Handling Objections

**"Too expensive"**
- Focus on time savings (70% reduction in admin workload)
- Compare to cost of incidents and liability
- Offer ROI calculator

**"Too complex to implement"**
- Show 2-4 week timeline
- Highlight minimal IT requirements
- Offer white-glove onboarding

**"Privacy concerns"**
- Deep dive into Slide 9
- Offer security whitepaper
- Provide compliance certifications

**"AI accuracy questions"**
- Emphasize 85-90% accuracy with human oversight
- Show alternative suggestions feature
- Explain continuous learning

### Follow-Up Materials
After presentation, send:
1. PDF of slides
2. Recording link
3. Demo environment access
4. Case studies
5. Technical documentation
6. Pricing sheet
