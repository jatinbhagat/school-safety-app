## Email Best Practices

### Timing
- **Initial outreach:** Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-2pm
- **Follow-ups:** Wait 3 business days
- **Reminders:** 24 hours before scheduled event

### Personalization
- Always use recipient's name and school name
- Reference specific challenges they mentioned
- Tailor examples to their context (K-12, college, district size)

### Subject Line Tips
- Keep under 50 characters
- Use numbers when possible ("3 ways to improve safety reporting")
- Create urgency without being pushy
- A/B test different approaches

### Call to Action
- One clear CTA per email
- Make it easy to respond (provide specific options)
- Remove friction (direct links, pre-filled calendar invites)

### Tracking
- Use email tracking to see opens and clicks
- Note which templates get best response rates
- Adjust based on audience (administrators vs. IT staff)
